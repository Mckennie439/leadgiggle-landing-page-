Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

At LeadGiggle ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy under the Constitution of Kenya (Article 31) and the Data Protection Act, 2019. You may browse our website without sharing personal data. We only collect personal information when you request a strategy call, subscribe, or contact us.

1. What Information We Collect & Why

Business & Contact Details

  • Business Name, Your Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Location: Needed to respond to your strategy call request and reach you directly.
  • Type of Business: Helps us understand your industry before the call.

Marketing Background

  • Whether you've run paid ads before, which platforms, current or past ad spend, and how you'd describe your results: Helps us understand your current marketing situation so the conversation is relevant to where your business actually stands.

Goals & Timeline

  • What part of the system interests you, your primary goal, and how soon you're hoping to get started: Helps us prepare a strategy call suited to your objectives.

Call Scheduling

  • Preferred call date and preferred time of day: Needed to confirm a time that works for you.

Optional Info

  • Additional context you choose to share before the call: Helps us prepare more specifically.

This data is collected only with your consent and is used to prepare for your strategy call, follow up on your request, and enhance our services if you opt in.

2. When You Contact Us or Participate

Strategy call requests, consultations, or promotions may ask for the details listed above.

We use this to serve you better, follow up, and sometimes send updates if you've opted in.

3. Communications & Marketing

With your consent, we may email you about new services, insights, or offers. You can opt out anytime via the link in our emails or by contacting us below.

4. Data Security

We use secure procedures and SSL encryption to protect your data. While no system is foolproof, we follow best practices to keep your information safe.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

  • We never sell or rent personal data.
  • We share anonymized insights only with partners for analytical purposes.
  • Third-party sites linked from our platform may have different policies. We encourage you to review theirs.

6. Children's Data

In compliance with Section 33 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 without parental consent. If you believe we've collected data about your child, please contact us so we can remove it immediately. We include age-gating and parental consent where required.

7. Your Rights Under Kenyan Law

Under Article 31(c) & (d) of the Constitution, and Sections 19–24 of the Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

RightDescription
AccessRequest a copy of your data.
CorrectionUpdate or fix your information.
DeletionAsk for removal when data is no longer needed.
RestrictPause processing if you dispute accuracy or legality.
Consent WithdrawalYou can withdraw consent at any time.
ObjectOpt out of direct marketing or automated profiling.

To exercise your rights, contact us below. We aim to respond within legal timelines.

8. Data Retention & Transfer

We keep data only as long as necessary, for service delivery, legal reasons, or your consent. Cross-border transfers (e.g., data stored in cloud services) are protected and comply with Kenyan law, including required safeguards and legal agreements.

9. Consent & Policy Updates

By using our site or submitting data, you consent to this policy. We may update it occasionally; changes will be posted here. Continued use implies you accept them.
